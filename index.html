Encrypted Data Protocols Overview

  • Foundation of Digital Trust

    Modern encryption protocols establish trust in digital interactions by ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of data across all transmission and storage mediums.

  • Homomorphic Encryption Breakthrough

    Allows computation on ciphertext, generating encrypted results that, when decrypted, match operations performed on the plaintext. This enables secure outsourcing of data processing.

  • Collaborative Security

    MPC protocols enable multiple parties to jointly compute a function over their inputs while keeping those inputs private, revolutionizing sectors requiring confidential collaboration.

  • Quantum Preparedness

    With quantum computing advances, post-quantum cryptography ensures existing encrypted data remains secure against future quantum attacks, providing forward secrecy.
