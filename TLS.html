RFC 8446: TLS Protocol Version 1.3

Published: August 2018
Category: Standards Track
Authors: 15 Contributors
Abstract

This document specifies version 1.3 of the Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocol. TLS allows client/server applications to communicate over the Internet in a way that is designed to prevent eavesdropping, tampering, and message forgery.

TLS 1.3 is a major revision that improves upon the security, performance, and privacy of previous versions. Key changes include removing support for weak cryptographic primitives, encrypting more of the handshake, and improving resistance to downgrade attacks.
